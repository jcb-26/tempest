About Tempest

What is Tempest?

Tempest is a fun, competitive trading platform where Saint Mary's students buy and sell digital ownership of real campus landmarks and items. Items up for sale include the De La Salle Statue, MoA sculptures, a Gael Smiski, and dozens of other objects that SMC home.

Every item on the marketplace has a stock of one. When you buy something, only you own it, until you decide to put it back on the market.

How It Works

New Squallers start with ؏1,000 in their wallet (squalls are our in-app currency). You can earn more by attending campus events, referring friends, and participating in school activities. Spend your squalls on items in the marketplace and watch your collection grow.

Listed an item too low? Bought something at a steal? Resell it for whatever price you want. Other Squallers can buy it from you, and the squalls go straight into your wallet. The market sets the price.

The Leaderboard

Every Squaller is ranked by their squalls. Climb to the top by trading, spotting daily deals, and building a collection that grows in value over time.

Daily Deals

Every day at midnight, four random items get marked down by 25%. Discounts only apply when you actually buy the item, so adding to cart isn't enough.
